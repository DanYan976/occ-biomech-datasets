MVTA - Multimedia Video Task Analysis

University of Wisconsin-Madison

Video task analysis software developed by Radwin and Yen at UW-Madison and distributed commercially by NexGen Ergonomics: analysts mark event breakpoints in video played at any speed or direction, and MVTA computes time studies, event frequencies, duty cycles and postural analyses. With its companion single-frame duty-cycle tool (SVDA) it supplied the duty-cycle measurements behind the frequency- and hand-speed-based ACGIH HAL equations.

Commercial

KineVid Computer-Vision HAL & Lifting Monitor

University of Wisconsin-Madison

UW-Madison spin-off commercializing Radwin's computer-vision exposure assessment: automatic ACGIH hand activity level from tracked hand motion in ordinary video, and a single-camera monitor that extracts lifting risk parameters aligned with the NIOSH lifting equation. Both are patent-protected (US 9,566,004 assigned to KineVid; US 11,450,148 assigned to WARF) and build on the hand speed-duty cycle HAL equation.
