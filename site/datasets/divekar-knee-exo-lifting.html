divekar-knee-exo-lifting Open Exo evaluation

Versatile Knee Exoskeleton: Lifting, Lowering & Carrying Fatigue Dataset

Divekar N., Thomas G., Yerva A., Frame H., Gregg R.

University of Michigan · 2024 · United States

Data behind Divekar et al. (2024, Science Robotics): 10 able-bodied subjects performed lifting, lowering, carrying, walking, ramp and stair tasks with and without a backdrivable knee exoskeleton. Includes normalized quadriceps and hamstring EMG, leg/knee kinematics, exoskeleton torque, ground reaction forces, task performance and perceptual ratings.
