zindashti-nekspine-neck-flexion Restricted Exo evaluation

NekSpine Neck and Lower-Back Exoskeleton: Sustained Neck-Flexion EMG and Endurance

Jasimi Zindashti N., Riahi N., Miller L., Tavakoli M., Rouhani H., Golabchi A.

University of Alberta / EWI Works · 2026 · Canada

Evaluation of the NekSpine passive neck + lower-back exoskeleton during sustained downward-gaze work: 10 participants (5M/5F) performed a simulated sorting task at 15-60° neck flexion and an endurance hold with and without the device while bilateral neck/back EMG and perceived exertion were recorded (Sensors, 2026). One of the few neck-support records in the catalog; data available upon request.
