Upper-Limb Exoskeleton on a Meat-Processing Production Line

Defani J.

Universidade Tecnológica Federal do Paraná (UTFPR) · 2025 · Brazil

Field study of a passive upper-limb exoskeleton worn by 17 workers on a Brazilian meat-processing production line: processed surface-EMG and subjective workload data with vs. without the device, reporting a significant activity reduction in the right medial deltoid and medium-to-large effect sizes across several muscles. One of very few open-data field studies of occupational exoskeletons; distributed as a single Excel workbook on Zenodo (CC BY 4.0).

About this catalog

This is a discovery index for the occupational biomechanics community — human-motion datasets today, with models and open-source analysis tools being added. Each entry describes a resource and links to its original source; listing is descriptive and is not an endorsement. Datasets keep their own licenses, and access terms are set by their authors.
