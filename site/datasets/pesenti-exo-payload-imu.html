pesenti-exo-payload-imu Open

IMU Activity Recognition & Payload Estimation for Low-Back Exoskeletons

Pesenti M.

Politecnico di Milano · 2022

IMU recordings from 12 subjects lifting and lowering boxes of 5-15 kg, pre-split into train/validation/test for intention (activity) classification and payload estimation, intended for low-back exoskeleton control. Sensors on chest, wrists and thighs; no exoskeleton was worn during collection.
