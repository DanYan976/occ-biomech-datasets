lim-vt-mmh-ergo-risk Open

Kinematic and EMG Dataset for Ergonomic Risk Assessment in Manual Material Handling

Lim S., Nussbaum M.A.

Virginia Tech · 2026 · United States

40 younger and older adults (20F/20M) performed simulated lifting/lowering and pushing/pulling tasks with several load types, masses, heights and handling depths, recorded simultaneously with IMUs, a Kinect and an EMG armband. One CSV per participant and modality, with trial conditions and demographics in every row. Built for training and benchmarking ML ergonomic-risk models across age and sex.

About this catalog
