lim-vt-mmh-ergo-risk Open

Kinematic and EMG Dataset for Ergonomic Risk Assessment in Manual Material Handling

Lim S., Nussbaum M.A.

Virginia Tech · 2026 · United States

40 younger and older adults (20F/20M) each completed 12 trials of lifting, cart pushing/pulling and lowering with three load types and five masses, including fatigue trials at the start and end, recorded with IMUs, three Azure Kinects and an EMG armband (14.9 h in total). Every sample carries activity labels and trial conditions. Built for training and benchmarking ML ergonomic-risk models across age and sex.

Details

Subjects
n = 40 · 20F / 20M · Younger (mean 23 ± 3.1 y) and older (mean 55.5 ± 5.3 y) adults
Setting
Laboratory (controlled or simulated)
Protocol / load
Horizontal box, vertical box and sandbag; 2.3, 4.5, 6.8, 9.1 and 11.3 kg; lifted from floor (0 m) or waist (0.90 m) to a cart, cart pushed/pulled 1.59 m, then lowered to shelves
Equipment
Noraxon Ultium Motion IMUs (16 sensors, joint angles); 3 Microsoft Azure Kinect depth cameras (markerless skeleton); Mindrove 8-channel EMG armband on the right forearm (raw + MVC-normalized)
Sampling
imu 200 Hz · emg 500 Hz · depth 30 Hz

csv

About this catalog
