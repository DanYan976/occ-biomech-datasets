Shoulder-Assist Exoskeleton Load-Carrying Walking and Lifting Stability Dataset

Chen Y., Ratnakumar N., Rodriguez M., Tohfafarosh M., Zheng L., Pan C., Zhou X., Yin W.

New Jersey Institute of Technology (AnyMotion Lab) / NIOSH · 2026

Whole-body optical motion capture and force-plate recordings of 11 adults performing a simulated MMH sequence — floor lift, carry while walking, shelf placement, retrieval and return of a 13.6 kg box — without an exoskeleton and wearing three commercial passive shoulder-assist exoskeletons. Collected in 2024 to characterize how shoulder exoskeletons affect balance across the full task: phase-specific margin of stability and sample entropy of the body+load CoM during walking, and CoP time-to-boundary during box placement. Box tracked as a rigid body; OpenSim-compatible. Release planned.
