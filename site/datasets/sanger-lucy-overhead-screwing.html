sanger-lucy-overhead-screwing Restricted Exo evaluation

Lucy Active Shoulder Exoskeleton: Overhead Screwing at Four Support Levels

Sänger J., Yao Z., Schubert T., Wolf A., Molz C., Miehling J., Wartzack S., Gwosch T., Matthiesen S., Weidner R.

KIT / Helmut Schmidt University Hamburg / FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg · 2022 · Germany

Rich multimodal evaluation of the Lucy active pneumatic shoulder exoskeleton: 5 participants performed two overhead screwing tasks at four support levels while 8-muscle EMG, optical mocap, ground reaction forces, interface pressure and tool current were recorded (Applied Sciences, 2022). Data available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
