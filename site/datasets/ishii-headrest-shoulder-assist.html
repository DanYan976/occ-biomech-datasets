ishii-headrest-shoulder-assist Restricted Exo evaluation

Shoulder Assist Device with Movable Headrest: Overhead Holds and Simulated Harvesting

Ishii C., Hirasawa K.

Hosei University · 2022 · Japan

Evaluation of a movable headrest add-on for a passive shoulder assist device, targeting the neck strain that shoulder-only exoskeletons ignore during face-up overhead work: 5 males performed static overhead holds and a 10-minute simulated fruit-harvesting protocol while deltoid and sternocleidomastoid EMG and muscle stiffness were measured across headrest variants (Wearable Technologies, 2022). Data from the corresponding author on reasonable request.
