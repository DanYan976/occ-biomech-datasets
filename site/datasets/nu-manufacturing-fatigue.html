nu-manufacturing-fatigue Open

Wearable Multi-Level Physical Fatigue Prediction in Manufacturing

Northwestern University

Northwestern University · 2024

Wearable-sensor (heart rate, ECG, chest temperature, chest accelerometer/ gyroscope) and self-reported Borg data from 43 workers performing composite layup and wire-harnessing tasks with a weighted vest to induce fatigue, across five work segments plus rest, for multi-level fatigue prediction.
