NIOSH: Shoulder-Assist Exoskeletons During Block-Laying on a Simulated Mast Climber

Zheng L., Pan C.S., Wei L., Bahreinizad H., Chowdhury S., Ning X., Santos F.

NIOSH / Texas Tech University · 2024 · United States

Processed shoulder EMG and force-plate balance data from 7 construction workers laying 35-lb blocks at two heights on a simulated mast-climbing work platform, without a device and with three anonymized passive shoulder-assist exoskeletons (IJIE, 2024). Public-domain NIOSH dataset, preserved on ICPSR DataLumos with a parallel CDC STACKS record (cdc/208601).

About this catalog

Each entry describes a resource and links to its original source; listing is descriptive and is not an endorsement. Datasets keep their own licenses, and access terms are set by their authors.
