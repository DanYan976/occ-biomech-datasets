Cray X Active Back Exoskeleton: Erector Spinae EMG Across 11 Support Levels

Walter T., Stutzig N., Siebert T.

University of Stuttgart · 2023 · Germany

Dose-response evaluation of the Cray X generation-4 active back exoskeleton: 14 participants stoop-lifted a 15 kg box at support levels from 0 to 100% in 10% steps plus a no-device condition while bilateral erector spinae EMG was recorded (Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology, 2023). Rare graded-assistance dataset; raw data available from the authors without undue reservation.
