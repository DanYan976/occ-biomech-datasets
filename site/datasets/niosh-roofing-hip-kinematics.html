NIOSH: Hip Kinematics Under Two Pelvis Marker Configurations in Roofing Tasks

National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH)

NIOSH / CDC · 2023 · United States

Methods dataset comparing hip kinematics from two pelvis tracking-marker configurations (trochanter-based TTM vs. virtual-marker VPTM, CODA pelvis) while 7 participants mimicked standing and kneeling roofing tasks. Useful for marker-set selection and occlusion-robust pelvis tracking in occupational motion capture. Public-domain NIOSH research dataset RD-1062-2023-0 on CDC STACKS.
